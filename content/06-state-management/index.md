State management is where most real-world React bugs and performance problems come from, so interviewers use it to separate people who have shipped apps from people who have only read docs. Expect questions on choosing where state lives, Redux Toolkit internals, Context pitfalls, and scenario questions about re-renders in large dashboards and tables.
